<p>I live in a small village in a rural area. It's quiet and peaceful here, and I love it.</p>
<p>I'm aware that, as a freelancer, it's important to keep up to date, attend events and meet people. It's good for business, but it's also motivating. That's why I try to attend at least one event every month.</p>
<p>The problem is that London is the nearest place where I can find interesting events, which means that attending one takes up a whole day. I’m fine with that, and I think it’s worth it.</p>
<p>A couple of weeks ago, for example, I attended an A11y Together event. It was really interesting, and I met some lovely people. The event alone made the trip worthwhile.</p>
<p>But for me, coming from a place where nature and silence are everywhere, a day in London is much more than just attending an event.</p>
<p>There are a few places I always try to visit when I'm in London. One of them is the Barbican Estate. I find its ugliness strangely attractive! And despite being surrounded by tall buildings and busy streets, it's such a peaceful place.</p>
<p>Another favourite is Tate Modern. I don't think I've ever managed to visit all the rooms, and there's always something new to see. And the Turbine Hall! It's impressive, no matter what's on display.</p>
<p>I also enjoy just wandering around and getting lost, which sometimes takes me to places I'd never have thought of visiting.</p>
<p>This time, I ended up in Hatton Garden, London's Diamond District. I'd never been there before. Dozens of jewellery shops, most of them seemingly empty, bodyguards as big as wardrobes, luxury black cars... It felt a bit intimidating! I later learnt that it’s a historic jewellery district that grew in the late 19th century, after the diamond discoveries in South Africa.</p>
<p>And then there's always room for a little surprise. While having a coffee before the accessibility event, I looked out of the window and saw two police motorbikes with their blue lights flashing, followed by a police car and four black cars. The Diplomat! I’m watching this Netflix series at the moment, and that’s exactly how they travel everywhere. 😄</p>
<p>I know that for many people, this might all seem quite ordinary. But for me, stepping outside my usual environment makes even the smallest things feel extraordinary. I notice things I wouldn't normally see, and I come back feeling full of energy.</p>
<p>I love living in a small village surrounded by nature. But sometimes I wonder if it’s the best choice for a digital business.</p>
