<p>I'm Sergi Duran Nyssen, and I make accessible websites and documents and help teams do the same.</p>
<p>I've spent more than two decades designing and building websites. When I started, most websites were still built using tables. Then responsive web design emerged, and I quickly realised it was the way forward. I specialised in it and later moved to Cape Town to work as a responsive web design specialist.</p>
<p>After a short time in London I spent a few years running my own company in sunny <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empord%C3%A0">Empordà</a>, in the north-east of Catalonia, and then the UK, where I’ve worked for a couple of agencies and built dozens of websites, always with accessibility in mind, even before I knew the word for it.</p>
<p>Today, I focus fully on digital accessibility. I help organisations create websites and documents that work for everyone, run accessibility tests and audits, and train teams on to design and write accessibly.</p>
<h2>Why Nyssen?</h2>
<p>My website and project are called Nyssen, in memory of my grandfather, who was a professional photographer. He introduced me to the fascinating world of photography, and later, sadly, to the world of accessibility, when he lost his sight to glaucoma shortly after retiring.</p>
<p>Through him, I learned how challenging everyday life can be for a blind person. But I also learned something else: making things accessible doesn't have to be difficult. Sometimes, it simply starts with being aware of other people's circumstances and thinking about the people who will use our products and services.</p>
